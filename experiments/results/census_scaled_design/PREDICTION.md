# A prediction, written before layer 52's trial is read

12 November 2026, while layer 52's trial is still being written (its verdict does not exist; nothing of its lead has
been looked at, only how its text survives, from the answers written so far).

`ceilings.py` (this folder) gives, for every layer, how many of a neuron's write ceilings a push of 1 of the stream is.
At the thirteen layers where `break_rel` passes (39 to 51) that number lies between 1.76 and 2.76. At layer 38, which
failed, it is 3.14. At layer 57, whose probe of 29 October failed on its +-1 answers, it is 1.21.

The reading: the design tells a neuron from a random direction where its lower dose is about two ceilings, and not
where it is much more (38) or much less (57).

Layer 52's +-1 is 1.06 ceilings, the least of any layer. So the prediction is that **layer 52 fails**, and fails as
layer 57 did: the +-1 answers alone carry little or no lead (N2), whatever the whole reading does. Layers 53 to 55
(1.63 to 1.77) are near layer 51's 1.76 and may pass; 56 to 58 (1.48, 1.21, 1.25) should fail.

## What happened

Layer 52's trial was read at about 20:31Z the same evening (`experiments/results/census_neighbour_trials/L52_verdict.txt`).
It fails, and on N2: the +-1 answers alone give a lead of 0.603, replication 0.546 and 0.577 against a bar of 0.57, and
0.554 on the new questions against 0.56. Its whole reading passes strongly (lead 0.802) and so do the neurons the
controls cover: the lead is in the +-2 answers, which at this layer are 2.1 ceilings. The prediction held, in the way
it was made. Layers 53 to 58 were not trialled: by the rule fixed first, the walk stops at a failure.
